Privacy Policy – ForageID

Last updated: May 2026

1. Information We Collect

ForageID does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Photos you upload are sent directly to the AI analysis service and are not stored on our servers.

2. Camera & Photo Access

The app requests access to your camera and photo library solely for the purpose of plant identification. Images are processed in real time and are not retained.

3. In-App Purchases

ForageID Pro subscriptions are processed securely through Google Play. We do not store payment information.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Anthropic's Claude AI API for image analysis. Please review Anthropic's privacy policy at https://www.anthropic.com/privacy.
